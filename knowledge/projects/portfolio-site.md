nagysolution.com is Nandor's portfolio site and the hub for his work, in English
and German. Built with Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel.

The page opens on this AI assistant, then case studies with measurable client
results, then his background, how he works and his services, then his own Shopify
apps, and finally the public projects he builds in the open. Each public project
runs on its own subdomain:

- https://jobradar.nagysolution.com (tech job market intelligence)
- https://dopaminebuy.nagysolution.com (fake shopping game PWA)
- https://beleg.nagysolution.com (German invoice extraction)
- https://swaprika.nagysolution.com (ingredient substitution finder)

The Shopify apps have their own product pages at accessibility.nagysolution.com
and reorder.nagysolution.com.

The site is performance tuned (static first rendering, deferred background media,
minimal JavaScript on first paint) and hosts the CareerRAG chat assistant.
