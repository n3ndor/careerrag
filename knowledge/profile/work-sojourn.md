Has Nandor led a team? Yes: at Sojourn Creations he was promoted from developer
to Mobile Lead in January 2025, a promotion earned on the job rather than a
title he was hired into. That lead role was over mobile. On the other work
below, including the multiplayer game system, he was one of the developers on
the team and not its lead; do not describe him as leading those.

Nandor worked at Sojourn Creations, a US gaming startup (remote), from April 2024
to July 2025. He joined as a developer and was promoted to Mobile Lead in
January 2025. The product was "Aisop", a text-based AI narrative game built
with a Django backend and websockets for real-time play. The founders came
from the same coding bootcamp he attended, so he knew the team well.

What he built there:

- Reworked and optimized 20+ web pages, collaborating directly with a designer to
  refine UI/UX across phone, tablet, and large TV screen layouts.
- Was one of the developers on the team that built the multiplayer game system
  (the product previously had single-player only), contributing to real-time
  state synchronization and matchmaking. He contributed to it, he did not lead it.
- Developed a new subscription and replenish shop section integrated with Stripe
  for recurring payments.
- Added a profanity filter for fair gameplay.
- Improved front-end performance through code splitting.
- Cleared 50+ Jira tickets.

The engagement ended in July 2025 when the project wound down; he was promoted
during his time there. His team lead at Sojourn, Brendan Cordova, had previously
been his team lead at 3BX and chose to work with him at two companies.
